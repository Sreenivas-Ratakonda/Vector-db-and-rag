# Brewly Coffee Co. - Refund and Returns Policy

## Eligibility
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unopened, except when the product arrived damaged or the wrong item was shipped.

## Damaged or wrong items
If a bag of beans or a machine arrives broken, crushed, or leaking, the customer should send a photo to support@brewly.example within 7 days. We reimburse the full amount including shipping. No need to return the damaged item.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Store credit is issued instantly if the customer prefers it, and comes with a 10 percent bonus.

## Subscriptions
Coffee subscriptions can be paused or cancelled any time before the next billing date. Already-shipped subscription boxes are not refundable unless damaged.

## Non-refundable items
Gift cards, opened bags of ground coffee, and items bought on clearance cannot be returned.
