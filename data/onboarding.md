# Brewly Coffee Co. - New Joiner Onboarding Guide

## Your first day
Collect your laptop and ID badge from the IT desk on the 2nd floor at 9:30 AM. Your buddy will take you for a coffee tasting session at 11 AM.

## First week checklist
Complete the mandatory security training, set up MFA, read the leave policy, and book a 1:1 with your manager. Join the #new-joiners Slack channel to ask questions.

## Expenses and reimbursement
Submit work expenses such as travel, client lunches, or equipment through the Expensify app within 30 days, with a photo of the receipt. Approved expenses are paid with your next monthly salary.

## Office facilities
The office is open from 7 AM to 9 PM on weekdays. Free coffee from every Brewly roast is available in the kitchen on each floor. Meeting rooms are booked through Outlook.

## Who to contact
HR questions go to hr@brewly.example. IT problems go to the IT helpdesk. Payroll questions go to payroll@brewly.example.
