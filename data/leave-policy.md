# Brewly Coffee Co. - Employee Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees get 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; anything beyond that expires on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences longer than 2 consecutive days, a doctor's note is required.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid leave. Non-birthing parents receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time in the first year.

## Applying for leave
Apply through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned time off. Your direct manager approves the request. Emergency leave can be applied for after the fact, within 3 working days.

## Public holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays. The list for the year is published on the HR portal every December.
