# Brewly Coffee Co. - IT Security Guidelines

## Passwords and login
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) using the authenticator app is mandatory for email, the HR portal, and the VPN. Never reuse your work password on other websites.

## Lost or stolen devices
If your laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the IT helpdesk immediately at it-help@brewly.example or extension 4357. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. Speed matters: report within 1 hour if possible.

## Phishing
Do not click links in unexpected emails asking for your password or payment details. Use the "Report Phish" button in Outlook. The security team never asks for your password.

## Remote work
Always connect through the company VPN when working from cafes, airports, or any public Wi-Fi. Lock your screen when stepping away (Windows key + L).

## Software installs
Only install software from the company Software Center. Requests for other tools go through the IT helpdesk and usually take 2 business days.
