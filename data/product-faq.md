# Brewly Coffee Co. - Product FAQ

## Which roast should I choose?
Light roast is bright and fruity with more acidity, ideal for pour-over. Medium roast is balanced and sweet, our best seller for drip machines. Dark roast is bold and smoky, best for espresso and milk drinks.

## How should I store coffee beans?
Keep beans in an airtight, opaque container at room temperature, away from sunlight. Do not store them in the fridge, because moisture and odours ruin the flavour. Beans taste best within 4 weeks of the roast date.

## What grind size do I need?
Coarse grind for French press and cold brew, medium for drip machines, fine for espresso, and extra fine for Turkish coffee.

## Does the Brewly Home Espresso machine need descaling?
Yes. Descale every 2 months with the Brewly descaling liquid, or every month if you live in a hard-water area. The orange light on the machine blinks when descaling is due.

## Is the decaf really caffeine-free?
Our Swiss Water decaf removes 99.9 percent of caffeine without chemicals. A cup contains roughly 2 mg of caffeine compared with about 95 mg in regular coffee.
